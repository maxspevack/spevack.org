# Privacy Policy

*Effective 2026-10-31.*

This page exists because Google requires one. It covers **Max Workspace**, the Google OAuth application behind the personal tools Max Spevack runs on his own machines, and nothing else on this site.

## What the tools are

Max Workspace is not a product or a service. It is the credential that lets two pieces of software Max wrote or operates for himself read and write his own Google account: a Model Context Protocol server that gives his AI tooling access to his Calendar, Gmail, and Drive, and a task manager that mirrors his to-do list onto a calendar of his own.

## Whose data

Only Max's. The application is authorized against his own Google account and no one else's. It has one user, and he is also the operator.

## What is accessed and why

Calendar, Gmail, and Drive data in Max's account, read and written on his instruction, for his own use. Nothing is collected for analytics, advertising, profiling, or training.

## Storage and sharing

Data stays on the machines Max owns and in his Google account. Nothing is copied to third parties, sold, or shared. OAuth tokens are kept in the operating system's encrypted credential store on each machine.

## Google Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
